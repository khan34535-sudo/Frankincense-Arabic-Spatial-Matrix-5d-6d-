What do 3D, 4D, 5D and 6D mean?

Each number adds one more kind of information to the map and the tree.

3D · Place

Three directions: east–west, north–south, and how far you zoom in.

4D · Time

Adds time. Slide through the months to see mist, harvest and leaves change.

5D · Light

Adds a special camera. See the world in heat, ultraviolet or X-ray colours.

6D · Contents

Adds what the resin is made of. Pick an ingredient to light it up.

Words to know

Resin
A sticky sap that hardens when it meets air. Frankincense is a resin.
Tapping
Making small cuts in the bark so the resin can flow out.
Incense Route
The old trade road that carried frankincense by camel from southern Arabia to the Mediterranean.
Khareef
The summer monsoon mist that waters the hills of Dhofar.
